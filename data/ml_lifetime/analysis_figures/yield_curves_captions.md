## Figure S1  (yield_curves_p_ArLi.png)

Solid lines are predictions from the globally-fit 5-parameter Arrhenius model yield = y_max·(1−exp(−k_f·tR))·exp(−k_d·tR), with k_f = exp(lnA_f − E_{a,f}/RT) and k_d = exp(lnA_d − E_{a,d}/RT). Red subplot borders mark compounds with fewer than 3 measured temperatures (reduced model reliability).
